DEVIATION FROM THE BRIEF, FLAGGED FOR IRIS/RED:
Iris specified the badge scaled uniformly to 1.6x (320 -> 512 CSS px).
Measured directly: the real panel with the expander open is 588 CSS px
tall (header through the disclaimer), all of it required content under
her own section 03 rule ("Never remove the ca., the data meter, the note
or the disclaimer to make the panel look tidier"). At 1.6x that is 941 CSS
px tall against a fixed 600 px stage -- no crop alignment (top, centre or
bottom) shows the whole panel, and every alignment tested loses either the
euro figures, the wordmark, or the plate tag and disclaimer she explicitly
protects. Rather than hide real DOM to force the 1.6x crop to fit, this
capture uses the largest scale that shows the complete, unedited panel
within the 600 px stage: 600/588 = ~1.02x (512 becomes ~328 CSS px wide).
Nothing is hidden or cropped. This is a deliberate deviation from the
literal 1.6x figure, made because that figure was specified before the
panel had actually been measured; happy to redo at a different scale if
Iris wants the crop instead.
